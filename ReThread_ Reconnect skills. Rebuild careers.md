## A career break shouldn't become a career barrier

Women often step away for marriage, motherhood or caregiving. Coming back is harder than leaving, and the usual options don't help.

The missing link: there is no bridge between "I used to have these skills" and "I am ready to work again." ReThread is that bridge.

### Skills go rusty

After 2 to 5 years away, skills fade and confidence drops.

### Gaps read as red flags

Hiring systems often treat an employment gap as a reason to say no.

### Participation stays low

India's female labour-force participation is far below men's.

### Upskilling doesn't fit

Long courses lose people, generic content misses personal gaps, certificates promise no work, and job portals don't fix "I'm rusty."

## We don't teach from zero

What other platforms ask

**"What do you want to learn?"**

Learn, get a certificate, search, apply, then earn. Generic content starts from zero.

What ReThread asks

**"What did you already know, and what needs refreshing?"**

Assess, learn, apply, earn. Every path is shaped by your past experience, skill gap and target role.

Confidence comes from small wins: daily micro-tasks, streaks and real projects that make progress visible.

## Years of interruption, turned into weeks of structured re-entry

### For women

- 10 to 15 minutes of learning a day
- More confidence from visible progress
- Higher employability, with skills tied to opportunities
- Earlier income from micro-gigs before full-time work

### For companies

- A vetted talent pool with verified skill progress
- Less screening effort than a resume gap alone
- A diverse pipeline of experienced returning professionals

### Buildable now, in three phases

**Phase 1: MVP**

- Resume parsing and 2-minute quiz
- Rule-based plus AI skill mapping
- Preset micro-learning modules
- Streaks, XP and skill dashboard

**Phase 2**

- Skill-to-opportunity matching
- Gig and internship matching

**Phase 3**

- AI personalization
- Content and collaborative filtering
- Employer dashboard

Stack: MongoDB, Express, React and Node.js for web, Flutter for mobile, Firebase for real-time sync and notifications, collaborative filtering for matching.
